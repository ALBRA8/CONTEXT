# RELATIONSHIP MAP — ALBRA WAVE 2

## Seed → specialized agents

`AGENTE-GENERAL` is the generic universal technical/operational seed. Specialized systems add domain capabilities rather than rewriting the universal organism.

## Research family

- `AGENTE-CHISMOSO`: broad investigation/intelligence.
- `AGENTE-LEADS`: B2B prospecting with deterministic evidence pipeline.
- `RADAR-SECOP2`: public-procurement opportunity intelligence with strong evidence/truth controls.
- `NEX-SCOPE`: YouTube niche discovery and market intelligence.

These systems share research/evidence patterns but are not interchangeable domains.

## Commercial/product family

- `AGENTE-KREA`: Product Factory / Commercial Product Engine.
- `CRM-ALBRA`: commercial/customer management and durable sales automations.
- `AGENTE-LEADS`: upstream lead intelligence and optional CRM hook.

## Audiovisual family

- Creative Engine → decides WHAT to create.
- Niche Blueprint → defines niche-specific grammar/rules.
- Reverse Engineering Engine → reconstructs production grammar.
- YOUTUBE-AUTOMATION → decides HOW to execute audiovisual production.
- HANDS → physical execution layer for YOUTUBE-AUTOMATION.
- `NEX-SCOPE` → discovery/market intelligence input.
- `Extension-CAZADOR` → separate capture/research utility; exact relationship to Flow extension remains to verify.

## Legal family

- `DESPACHO-JURIDICO` → legal organism base.
- Proposed specialist pool → Director Jurídico, IP/Copyright, Legal Case/Contract, Evidence/Provenance, Legal Gate.
- Audiobook rights research → candidate workload for legal domain, not automatically Krea/Chismoso/Leads.

## Global control / memory

- `CONTEXT` → external versioned continuity/documentation memory.
- EEC/Mission Control → proposed visual global control plane.
- EEC is not a super-agent.

## Architectural invariant

A capability appearing in one system does not automatically justify moving it to another. New responsibilities must be classified as transversal, domain, integration, or new-specialist work before assignment.
