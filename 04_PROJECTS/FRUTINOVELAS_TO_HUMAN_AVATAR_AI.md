`PROMPT MAESTRO FRUTINOVELAS V2.0 — SISTEMA OPERATIVO`

## Transformation

The narrative engine of Frutinovelas is retained, while the visual and production architecture is replaced to create:

**NOVELAS — HUMAN AVATAR AI**

The objective is not to reproduce the original Frutinovelas visual identity. The transformation keeps the useful narrative machinery and changes the representation/production layer.

## Preserved layer

- narrative engine and its useful storytelling mechanics;
- structured scene/narrative organization where applicable;
- continuity requirements needed by the narrative system.

## Replaced layer

The visual/production architecture is completely reoriented toward realistic human-avatar production.

The target direction includes:

- hyperrealistic human avatars;
- human-performed visual language rather than fruit-character identity;
- detailed scene/image prompting;
- explicit environmental/background specification;
- explicit camera-position specification;
- continuity of avatar appearance and scene state.

## Approved production ecosystem for this direction

The working direction specified for this transformation is to use only:

- Google Flow;
- Meta AI / Vibes.

Other visual-generation ecosystems should not be introduced into this framework unless a later decision changes the scope.

## Naming/content transformation

Fruit-specific names/identities are not treated as mandatory inherited content. The transformed system uses new names/identities appropriate to human-avatar novelas.

The transformation therefore follows:

`FRUTINOVELAS narrative engine → HUMAN AVATAR narrative system`

rather than:

`FRUTINOVELAS characters → humanized copies`.

## Prompt-preservation rule

When generating image/scene prompts for the transformed system, preserve the level of useful visual specificity established in the original workflow, including at minimum the relevant:

- subject/avatar description;
- environment;
- background;
- camera position;
- shot/scene context;
- continuity information.

## Architectural significance

The transformation demonstrates a broader ALBRA framework principle:

**Narrative architecture and visual/production architecture can evolve independently.**

A framework may preserve its useful reasoning/narrative engine while replacing the implementation layer required by a new production medium.
