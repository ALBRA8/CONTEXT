# REVERSE ENGINEERING ENGINE V1.2 — TEST B / TRANSFER

## Evidence status

`TEST / ARCHITECTURAL REFERENCE`

This record preserves the transfer test performed during the ALBRA design work. It is not a copy of the reference video, its story, characters, dialogue, sequence or twist. It records the transferable production grammar extracted for reuse.

## Test objective

Use only the result of TEST A from a reference video as the transfer base and apply the reconstructed production grammar to a completely new idea, without reusing the original content.

## New idea used in TEST B

Two workers are remodeling a small neighborhood store. While removing an apparently normal shelf, they discover a hidden room behind the wall.

## Transfer rule

The transferable layer is the **grammar of production**, not the source content.

Do not copy:

- original characters;
- original relationships;
- original dialogue;
- original setting/content identity;
- original sequence of events;
- original twist or payoff;
- recognizable story-specific details.

## Transferred production grammar

The TEST B transfer preserved these structural characteristics:

1. **Human / environmental realism** — actions and reactions should feel physically and socially natural rather than staged.
2. **Spatial continuity** — maintain coherent geography of the location and progression through the space.
3. **Character continuity** — preserve each worker's appearance, clothing and physical state across shots.
4. **Object continuity** — shelves, tools, debris and discovered elements must remain consistent with prior actions.
5. **Documentary handheld language** — camera movement should feel observational and physically motivated rather than artificially cinematic.
6. **Functional coverage** — shots exist to clearly communicate the work being performed, the environment and the discovery.
7. **Physical-action progression** — events advance through concrete actions rather than exposition alone.
8. **Escalation** — the ordinary remodeling task progressively becomes more consequential.
9. **Complication** — the discovery introduces an unexpected problem/question that changes the situation.
10. **Human reactions** — characters visibly react to what happens; reactions are part of the narrative information.
11. **Payoff** — the hidden-room discovery provides a concrete reveal that resolves the immediate escalation while opening the appropriate narrative consequence.
12. **Rhythm / language** — pacing follows physical discovery and human reaction, maintaining the naturalistic grammar of the transferred format.

## Architectural significance

This test establishes a reusable distinction:

`REFERENCE VIDEO → RECONSTRUCTED GRAMMAR → NEW IDEA → ORIGINAL PRODUCTION`

The Reverse Engineering Engine is therefore a **structure/grammar reconstruction mechanism**, not a content-copying mechanism.

This record provides the concrete test evidence behind the framework rule that Reverse Engineering reconstructs grammar/structure while generated content remains original.
