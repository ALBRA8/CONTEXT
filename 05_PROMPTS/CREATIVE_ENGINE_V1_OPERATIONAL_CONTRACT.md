## Role

The Creative Engine is responsible for deciding **WHAT** should be created within the applicable creative/niche constraints.

YOUTUBE-AUTOMATION is responsible for deciding **HOW** that approved creative specification is executed as audiovisual production.

`CREATIVE ENGINE = WHAT`

`YOUTUBE-AUTOMATION = HOW`

## MODE A — CREATE

The framework supports the creation mode used in ALBRA tests: develop an original creative proposal from the supplied idea while respecting the applicable Niche Blueprint and Creative Engine rules.

## Required output architecture

The creative workflow produces the structured production definition used downstream, including:

- `CREATIVE PRODUCTION SPECIFICATION`;
- `PRODUCTION JSON`.

The exact schemas/rules remain governed by the original Creative Engine source and the applicable Niche Blueprint.

## Niche Blueprint constraint

When a Niche Blueprint is supplied, it is authoritative for the niche-specific creative grammar and constraints.

The engine must not invent additional rules and present them as if they came from the Blueprint.

## Originality / non-copying rule

When a reference work is supplied for inspiration or structural analysis, the generated production must not literally copy the reference's:

- story;
- characters;
- relationships;
- dialogue;
- twist/payoff;
- sequence of events;
- distinctive story-specific content.

Structural/grammatical learning is distinct from content copying and is handled explicitly by the Reverse Engineering framework where applicable.

## Source hierarchy

For an execution request, distinguish:

1. the exact Creative Engine V1.0 source;
2. the applicable Niche Blueprint;
3. the user's current idea/instructions;
4. downstream production constraints.

Do not silently replace the original engine with a reconstructed summary.

## Relationship to Reverse Engineering

Creative Engine and Reverse Engineering are complementary but distinct:

- Creative Engine creates within the creative framework.
- Reverse Engineering reconstructs transferable grammar/structure from a reference.
- Neither authorizes copying reference-specific content.

## Architectural significance

The separation prevents creative reasoning from being conflated with audiovisual execution. A creative specification can therefore be passed to YOUTUBE-AUTOMATION without making the audiovisual orchestrator responsible for inventing the creative framework itself.
